# Meeting walkthrough — SELCA COMPANY demo (≈ 15 minutes)

Setup: two browser windows side by side — left: storefront `http://localhost:5173`, right: CMS `http://localhost:5173/admin`
(already logged in). Run "Resetuj demo" in Postavke beforehand so the numbers are fresh.

## 1. First impression (2 min) — storefront home
- Full-screen hero slider ("Sve za vaš dom.") with their roof-logo, red brand colour, three languages in the top bar.
- Scroll: trust bar (besplatno mjerenje, stručna ugradnja, garancija, dostava) → six categories → bestsellers →
  autumn promo with live countdown and coupon → "kako radimo" in 4 steps → services → projects → FAQ → Instagram
  (@selca_doo) → **free-measurement booking form**.
- Switch the language to **Shqip** and back — everything translates, including product data.

## 2. Shopping like a customer (4 min)
- Products → **Podovi** → filters (price, colour, on sale, with installation).
- Open a laminate: gallery, **m² calculator** (type 34 m² → packs incl. 10 % waste), **"dodaj ugradnju"**,
  live total, delivery & installation tab.
- Open a kitchen: made-to-measure → **"Zatraži ponudu"** form instead of a cart button.
- Cart drawer → free-delivery progress bar → coupon `SELCA10` → checkout (pouzećem / uplata na račun / kartica)
  → order confirmation with the order number.

## 3. The "wow" moment (1 min)
- The order appears **instantly** in the CMS window: notification bell, toast, dashboard numbers update.
- The measurement request from the form appears under **Upiti i mjerenja** — schedule the visit right there.

## 4. Running the business in the CMS (5 min)
- **Kontrolna tabla** — revenue, orders, average basket, top products, low stock, upcoming measurements.
- **Narudžbe** — open the new order → move it through Potvrđena → U pripremi → Poslata → Ugradnja → Završena,
  add a note, print the invoice/pro-forma.
- **Proizvodi** — edit a product: three-language texts, photos (upload from phone), variants with price
  differences, specs, installation price, SEO preview → save → refresh the storefront: changed.
- **Početna stranica** — the homepage builder: edit the hero headline, reorder sections, hide one — the live
  preview updates as you type (desktop / tablet / mobile).
- **Postavke** — company data, shipping zones and prices, payment methods, languages, **brand colour** (try a
  preset → the whole site recolours), export / reset data.

## 5. Close (2 min)
- Everything they saw is editable by their own staff, in Montenegrin or Albanian.
- Production version: their domain (selcacompany.com is currently down), real product photos, online card
  payments via a Montenegrin bank gateway, Google indexing in three languages, e-mail notifications for new
  orders and measurement requests.

Tip: if anything gets messy during the demo, **Postavke → Demo podaci → Resetuj demo** restores everything in one click.
